About ShareMeal

ShareMeal is a food waste reduction and donation platform that connects food donors with people in need.

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce food waste and make surplus food available to underprivileged people.

How It Works

1. Donors list their surplus food.

2. NGOs or receivers find available food.

3. Food is collected and distributed to people in need.
